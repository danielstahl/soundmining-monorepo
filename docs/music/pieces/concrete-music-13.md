# Wooden clouds

This is the thirteenth piece in a series of Concrete Music pieces. The sounds come from short wood hits. 
The music is generative. The source-code can be found at [github.com/danielstahl/soundmining-monorepo/tree/main/projects/concrete-music-13](https://github.com/danielstahl/soundmining-monorepo/tree/main/projects/concrete-music-13)
