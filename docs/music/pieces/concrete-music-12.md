# Shadows from points

This is the twelfth piece in a series of Concrete Music pieces. The sounds are shadows of the sound in the eleventh piece, Points in isolation. 
The music is generative. The source-code can be found at [github.com/danielstahl/soundmining-monorepo/tree/main/projects/concrete-music-12](https://github.com/danielstahl/soundmining-monorepo/tree/main/projects/concrete-music-12)
