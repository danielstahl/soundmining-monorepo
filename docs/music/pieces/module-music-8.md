# Electric Veins
This is the eighth in a series of Module Music pieces. It uses fm-synthesis. It is based on a spectrum and sub-spectrum between A4 and F#5. The music is generative and uses both  markov chains and random ranges. The source-code can be found at [github.com/danielstahl/soundmining-monorepo/tree/main/projects/module-music-8](https://github.com/danielstahl/soundmining-monorepo/tree/main/projects/module-music-8)
