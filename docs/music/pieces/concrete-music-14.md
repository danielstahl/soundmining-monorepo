# Dots and Strokes

This is the fourteenth piece in a series of Concrete Music pieces. The sounds come from pots and wooden bricks. 
There are four short movements. The music is generative. 
The source-code can be found at [github.com/danielstahl/soundmining-monorepo/tree/main/projects/concrete-music-14](https://github.com/danielstahl/soundmining-monorepo/tree/main/projects/concrete-music-14).
