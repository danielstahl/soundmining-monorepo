Electric Veins is the eighth in a series of Modular Music pieces. It uses fm-synthesis. It is based on a spectrum and sub-spectrum between A4 and F#5. 
The music is generative and uses both  markov chains and random ranges. It is written in Supercollider and mixed in Reaper. 
The video is edited in Da Vinci Resolve. The music is Dark Ambient, Electroacoustic, Generative music.
